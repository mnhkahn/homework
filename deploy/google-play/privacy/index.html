作业小伙伴隐私政策

本政策适用于 Cyeam 提供的作业小伙伴 Google Play 版（com.homeworkbuddy）。你可以直接体验本机示例。家长可以选择关联个人 Trello 账号和私有作业看板。

设备上的数据

应用在设备上保存你填写的昵称、作业缓存、计时和完成进度、家长设置及待提交成果。拍摄作业时，应用使用相机并在设备上裁剪和处理照片。选择录音时，应用使用麦克风或系统录音工具。示例的作业完成状态只保存在本机，示例答案和媒体不会发送。连接 Trello 后，应用可以在获得系统通知许可时显示作业提醒。

家长启用 Trello 后

家长在浏览器中的 Trello 页面登录和授权。应用使用受 Android Keystore 保护的授权码，通过 HTTPS 从 Atlassian Trello 读取授权范围内的看板和作业资料，包括卡片、评论、批改结果及附件。需要时，应用在选定的看板中创建作业列表。应用将作业开始、完成时间及相关计时记录同步到看板。你确认提交的文字、照片、录音和适用的预习要求会发送到相应 Trello 卡片。拥有该私有看板访问权限的人可以查看这些内容，具体访问和留存由家长的 Trello 设置及 Atlassian 政策管理。Cyeam 的服务器不转发或保存这些作业数据和授权码。家长应管理看板成员，并在授权和提交前确认这些数据适合分享。

学习网页和在线发音

打开作业中的学习网页时，目标服务会收到连接所需信息，例如 IP 地址和请求地址。词汇和朗读功能在部分情况下会向有道词典或 Cyeam 的语音接口请求教材单词或句子的发音，这些服务会收到所请求的单词或句子及连接信息。请勿在用于在线发音的内容中填写私人信息。

保留、删除与联系

应用不创建 Cyeam 账号，也不包含广告、广告标识符收集、应用使用统计或崩溃报告 SDK。你可在 Android 设置中清除作业小伙伴的应用数据以移除本地记录和授权码。通过系统录音工具保存的音频可在该工具中删除。家长可在 Trello 撤销授权，并在 Trello 删除已同步的数据。在线发音请求用于生成或返回音频，网络服务可能按照其运维和安全需要保留请求日志。

家长和用户如需查询、更正或删除 Cyeam 能定位并控制的数据，或对本政策有疑问，可以联系 lichao@cyeam.com。请勿发送密码、Trello 授权码或包含儿童个人信息的附件。

Homework Buddy Privacy Policy

This policy covers Cyeam’s Homework Buddy app for Google Play (com.homeworkbuddy). You can try the on-device demo immediately. A parent can choose to connect their own Trello account and a private homework board.

Data on the device

The app stores the nickname you enter, cached assignments, timing and completion progress, parent settings, and pending submissions on the device. When you photograph homework, the app uses the camera and crops and processes the image on the device. If you choose to record audio, the app uses the microphone or your system recorder. Demo completion stays on the device. Demo answers and media are never sent. After you connect Trello, the app can show homework reminders if you grant Android notification permission.

When a parent enables Trello

The parent signs in and grants access on the Trello website in a browser. The app uses a token protected by Android Keystore and connects to Atlassian Trello over HTTPS to read authorized board and assignment information. That includes cards, comments, feedback, and attachments. The app creates assignment lists on the selected board when needed. It syncs assignment start and completion times and related timing records to the board. Text, photos, recordings, and applicable preparation requirements you confirm are sent to the corresponding Trello card. People with access to that private board can see the content. The parent’s Trello settings and Atlassian’s policies govern access and retention. Cyeam’s servers do not relay or store this homework data or the token. Parents should manage board membership and decide what is appropriate to share before authorizing access or submitting work.

Learning pages and online pronunciation

When you open a learning page from an assignment, the destination receives connection information such as your IP address and the requested URL. Vocabulary and reading features sometimes request pronunciation for lesson words or sentences from Youdao Dictionary or Cyeam’s speech service. Those services receive the requested words or sentences and connection information. Please keep private information out of material used for online pronunciation.

Retention, deletion and contact

The app does not create a Cyeam account and contains no advertising, advertising identifier collection, app usage analytics, or crash reporting SDK. Clear Homework Buddy’s app data in Android settings to remove its local records and token. Audio saved by a system recorder can be deleted in that recorder. Parents can revoke access in Trello and delete synced data there. Online pronunciation requests are used to generate or return audio. Network services may retain request logs for operations and security.

Parents and other users may contact lichao@cyeam.com with questions or to request access, correction, or deletion of data that Cyeam can identify and controls. Please keep passwords, Trello tokens, and attachments containing children’s personal information private.
